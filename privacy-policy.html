Privacy Policy

Information about how IQRA English Medium School website handles visitor information.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the public website of IQRA English Medium School, operating under Limra Educational Society in Azad Nagar, Makthal, Dist. Narayanpet, Telangana – 509208. This policy provides information about how this website handles visitor information.

Information We Collect

This website is primarily informational. It does not currently provide user accounts, student or parent login functionality, online payments, online admissions or a contact submission form.

The website does not directly collect personal information from visitors through forms or registration processes. However, hosting providers or browsers may process limited technical information (such as IP addresses or device type) as part of standard web delivery.

Contact Information

The school's phone numbers, email address, physical address and Instagram information are displayed on this website so that visitors can contact the school directly using their preferred method.

If a visitor contacts the school through phone, email or an external platform such as Instagram, information shared through those services may be handled according to the relevant service's privacy practices and the school's normal communication practices.

Cookies and Analytics

The current website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or tracking technologies directly. However, hosting providers or embedded third-party services may process limited technical information necessary to provide their services.

If analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

External Links

This website may contain links to third-party services such as Instagram or map/directions services. IQRA English Medium School is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services. Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policies of any external services they use.

Children's Privacy

This public website is intended to provide general information about IQRA English Medium School. The website does not knowingly collect personal information from children through online forms or registration.

The school aims to handle photographs and information displayed on its website responsibly and with appropriate consideration for student privacy.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if website features or privacy practices change, such as the introduction of contact forms, analytics services, student/parent portals or other online services.

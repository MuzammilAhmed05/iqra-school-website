Our Story

Growing with a Purpose

Since 2009, IQRA English Medium School has been serving young learners in Makthal under Limra Educational Society. The school provides English-medium education from Nursery to 7th Class following the State Board curriculum.

Our focus is to provide children with a supportive environment where learning, confidence, character and good values can grow together.
